Full Subject-Based Banding (FSBB) — reference brief (MVP stub corpus)

Purpose: This stub document supports retrieval-augmented prompts in the FSBB comparator scenario. It is not an official MOE text.

1. Banding and posting
- Secondary 1 posting uses Achievement Levels (AL) from the PSLE.
- FSBB organises students into mixed-form classes while offering subject-level bands (G1, G2, G3) suited to learner readiness in individual subjects.

2. Implementation posture
- Schools balance parental expectations, teacher workload, and timetable constraints when assigning students to subject bands.
- Form teachers and HODs coordinate communication so families understand that banding is subject-specific, not a single “stream label” for the child.

3. Equity and stress
- Policy intent includes reducing fixation on a single high-stakes score while preserving rigour; schools monitor perceived stigma and support transitions between bands where appropriate.
